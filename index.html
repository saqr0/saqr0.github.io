How a night works

Drivers

Register online, pass the scrutineering check at the gate, then run in your group. Beginners and experienced drivers run separately.

Spectators

Watch from the marked viewing area behind the barriers. Food trucks and the pit walk open until the last run.

Instructors

New to drifting? Book a 20-minute ride-along or a coached session before the main event.

Track rules

  1. Helmet on whenever the car is moving. No exceptions.
  2. Check tyres, fluids and loose items before every session.
  3. Stay behind the barriers unless a marshal waves you through.
  4. Alcohol-free for drivers, all night.
